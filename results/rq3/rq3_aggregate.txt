QMon misses
-----------
QMon detects 1,782 and misses 1,078 of the 2,860 mutants. Of the misses, 635
have no downstream monitorable checkpoint, 11 have a monitorable checkpoint
that is not deployed under Qmax=24, and 432 have at least one deployed
checkpoint but produce no QMon flag. Among all misses, 345 are detected by at
least one baseline and 87 are missed by every evaluated method.
